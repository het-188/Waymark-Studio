Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or use our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data.

1. Who We Are

Waymark Studio is a digital and creative services studio based in India. We operate the website at waymarkstudio.in. For any privacy-related enquiries, contact us via Email.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Information you provide directly

When you fill out an enquiry form on our website, we ask for:

  • Your name
  • Your business name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Business type, services needed, budget range, and timeline (optional)
  • A message describing your enquiry

This information is transmitted via WhatsApp to our team and is not stored on any server operated by us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we may collect certain technical information automatically when you visit our site, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring URL, pages visited and time spent. This data is used only for improving site performance and understanding usage patterns.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your project enquiries and provide quotes
  • Communicate about project progress and deliverables
  • Manage our client relationships and service agreements
  • Improve the quality of our website and services

We do not use your information for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service delivery: Where necessary to deliver your project (for example, sharing access details with a trusted technical collaborator under confidentiality agreement).
  • Legal obligation: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfer: In the event that Waymark Studio is merged with or acquired by another entity, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

5. WhatsApp Communication

Our enquiry form transmits your information via WhatsApp (a Meta platform). By submitting the enquiry form, you consent to this data being sent through WhatsApp. The information you send is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy available at whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We recommend you do not include sensitive personal or financial information in your initial enquiry message.

6. Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We may use functional cookies to support basic site functionality (such as remembering your preferences). No third-party analytics scripts are currently deployed on this site.

If this changes in the future, this policy will be updated and a cookie notice will be displayed.

7. Data Retention

Enquiry information sent via WhatsApp is retained in our WhatsApp account for as long as necessary to respond to and manage client communications. Client project information (emails, files, contracts) is retained for at least 3 years after the end of the project for legal and accounting purposes.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us, subject to any retention requirements imposed by law.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via Email.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
